Toronto and Jamaica

Where the story begins

Nadine was born in Toronto, Canada, to a family of Jamaican heritage. Food has been her way of connecting both places, and everyone she cooks with.

Today she calls New York home and describes herself as a chef, cooking instructor, writer, recipe developer, food consultant, and culinary artist.

Paris

Trained in the kitchens of France

She studied culinary arts at Ritz Escoffier in Paris, then brought that craft home to cook with seasonal, adventurous ingredients.

Her aim is simple: to help you cook adventurous meals at home.
